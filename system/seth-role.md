# Seth Role

## Role Definition

Seth is building the Milwaukee market for Brillect by creating qualified pipeline, matching change-driven companies with Brillect services, and making Brillect a known, trusted advisory brand in the local business community.

## Primary Objective

Create qualified Milwaukee pipeline.

Brand awareness matters because it increases trust, referrals, warm introductions, and event gravity. The system should judge brand-building work by whether it creates or strengthens future pipeline.

## Pipeline Engine

The default pipeline motion is relationship-first:

1. Start with existing relationships.
2. Identify business change signals.
3. Map the relationship path to a senior buyer or credible influencer.
4. Use a helpful, curious conversation to surface urgency and budget.
5. Bridge toward an assessment-led advisory entry point when appropriate.
6. Capture outcomes so the system gets sharper over time.

## Relationship Priority Stack

Client-side buyers:

- CHROs
- CFOs
- PE sponsors

Channel-side multipliers:

- Attorneys, especially M&A, corporate, employment, private equity, and transaction counsel

Every pipeline recommendation should identify the relationship path first. If no credible relationship path exists, then use market research signals or colder outreach as secondary options.

## Primary Trigger Families

Overweight these trigger families for pipeline creation:

- M&A: acquisition, sale prep, diligence, integration, ownership transition, PE activity
- ERP/HCM: implementation, replacement, failed rollout, readiness, change management, process redesign, finance or HR system strain

Best-fit pipeline pattern:

`existing relationship + CHRO/CFO/PE sponsor or attorney + M&A or ERP/HCM trigger`

## Assessment-Led Direction

Seth's natural opener is a broad relationship conversation: "What are you seeing?"

The system should help graduate those conversations into assessment-led advisory entry points without sounding salesy. Start relationally, listen for trigger events, reflect the issue in the buyer's language, then offer a lightweight readiness check, risk scan, outside lens, or prioritization exercise.

Current assessment priority:

1. ERP/HCM readiness
2. Growth / business assessment
3. AI readiness
4. M&A assessment, with buy-side, integration, and post-close readiness easier than sell-side prep
