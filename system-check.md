# Brillect Workspace System Check

## Purpose

This workspace is the persistent Brillect AI Operating System for Seth Wooll's Milwaukee market work. It supports business development, pipeline discipline, channel partner follow-up, event scouting, meeting prep, and revenue-focused execution toward the 2026 Milwaukee goals.

## Available Skills

- `actuals-intake` - log weekly revenue and hours into actuals tracking.
- `briefer` - prepare for meetings, calls, and relationship conversations.
- `candidate-summary` - draft concise consultant summaries for clients.
- `conductor` - run the daily command briefing.
- `connector` - debrief meetings and convert them into follow-ups, signals, and pipeline actions.
- `daily-debrief` - close out the day or week and identify updates.
- `job-order-builder` - turn client needs into structured job orders.
- `profile-reviser` - tailor consultant profiles to specific roles.
- `sales-outreach` - draft personalized B2B outreach emails.
- `scout-events` - find Milwaukee/Wisconsin networking events.
- `weekly-scorecard` - summarize weekly activity and gaps.

## How To Use This Workspace

- Treat Markdown files in `data/` and `system/` as the source of truth, not chat history.
- Use the relevant skill when the work matches a repeatable workflow.
- Capture durable context in files: pipeline, follow-ups, channel partners, target accounts, market signals, actuals, and preferences.
- Before changing core data files, state what is being changed and why.
- Keep outputs concise, practical, and tied to revenue progress.
- Do not store passwords, API keys, private tokens, or sensitive financial credentials here.
