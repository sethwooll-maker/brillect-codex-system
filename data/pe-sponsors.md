# PE Sponsors

Tracks PE sponsor relationships, Wisconsin/Midwest portfolio companies, buy signals, and relationship paths.

Goal: Convert PE-backed change events into relationship-first pipeline moves for Brillect.

## How To Use

For each sponsor, track:

- Contact(s)
- Relationship path
- Portfolio companies relevant to Wisconsin/Midwest
- Acquisition dates and likely hold period
- Trigger signals
- Brillect angle
- Next move
- Priority

Ratings:

- A: urgent, senior path, clear Brillect fit
- B: real trigger, but urgency or budget unclear
- C: interesting, needs more relationship/signal work
- Nurture: relationship to maintain, not pipeline yet
