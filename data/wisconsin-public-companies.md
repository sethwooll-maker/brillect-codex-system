# Wisconsin Public Companies

Tracks Wisconsin-based public companies as a standing signal universe for Brillect pipeline creation.

Goal: Review public-company disclosures and news for M&A, integration, ERP/HCM, transformation, restructuring, leadership, SOX/public reporting, and finance/HR infrastructure signals.

## Scan Rules

During a full buy-signal scan:

1. Verify the current Wisconsin-headquartered public-company universe using current sources.
2. Review SEC filings, investor relations pages, earnings releases, leadership announcements, and press releases.
3. Prioritize companies with existing relationships, visible transformation triggers, M&A/integration activity, leadership change, or Big 4 fatigue entry points.
4. Convert each useful signal into a relationship move.
5. Do not treat public-company status as qualification by itself. Public companies are often harder to enter; require a specific workstream, buyer, or relationship path.

## Review Prompts

For each high-fit company, ask:

- What changed in the last 30-90 days?
- Is there M&A, integration, restructuring, leadership, ERP/HCM, or transformation activity?
- Is there a known CFO, CHRO, Controller, transformation, or integration buyer?
- Is there a relationship path through Seth, Brillect, Deloitte alumni, attorneys, bankers, PE, ACG, FEI, or channel partners?
- What is the most natural assessment bridge?
- Is the signal urgent enough to deserve action this week?

Agents should update this file when a company is added, removed, acquired, relocates, goes public, delists, or produces a verified buy signal.
