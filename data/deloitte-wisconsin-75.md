# Deloitte Wisconsin 75

Tracks the Deloitte Wisconsin 75 as a standing private-company signal universe for Brillect pipeline creation.

Source of truth: Deloitte Wisconsin 75 program page and latest honoree PDF.

Latest loaded list: 2025 Deloitte Wisconsin 75.

Why this matters:

- Large private Wisconsin companies often have growth, succession, ERP/HCM, leadership, M&A, and ownership-transition needs.
- The list includes family-owned, individual-owned, ESOP, and PE-backed companies that may be better fits for relationship-first advisory conversations than broad public-company outreach.
- Companies should not be treated as qualified solely because they appear here. Use this as a signal pool, then test trigger, relationship path, urgency, budget, and Brillect fit.

## Scan Rules

During a full buy-signal scan:

1. Review the newest Deloitte Wisconsin 75 list.
2. Search high-fit companies for M&A, leadership change, ERP/HCM, growth, facility expansion, succession, PE, and restructuring signals.
3. Prioritize companies with existing relationships or attorney/channel paths.
4. Convert any useful signal into a relationship move.
5. Recommend updates to `data/signals.md`, `data/followups.md`, and `data/target-accounts.md` only when the signal is actionable.
