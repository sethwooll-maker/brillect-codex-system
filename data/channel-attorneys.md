# Channel Attorneys

Tracks attorney relationships and law firm channel opportunities that can create Brillect pipeline.

Goal: Build value-first relationships with attorneys who see M&A, PE, employment, succession, restructuring, and transformation triggers before the market does.

## Priority Practice Areas

- M&A / corporate transactions
- Private equity
- Employment / labor
- Restructuring
- Estate, succession, and closely held business planning
- Technology, privacy, and transformation-adjacent counsel

## Touchpoint Standard

Monthly touchpoint for active attorney relationships. Lead with value first:

- market pattern Brillect is seeing
- event invite
- useful introduction
- short point of view on ERP/HCM, AI, growth, or integration readiness
- "what are you seeing?" conversation
- offer to be a sounding board for a client situation
